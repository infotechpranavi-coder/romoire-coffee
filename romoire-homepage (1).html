How to make it

A proper cappuccino.
From one sachet.

No frother. No milk. No machine. The froth comes from the sachet, not from equipment — which is the whole reason this works in a hotel room at six in the morning.

Tear

One sachet into your cup. 20g, pre-measured — nothing to spoon or weigh.

02

03

Stir

The froth builds as you go, then settles into a thick, even head.

Ask a question →

Does it really froth without milk or a frother?

Yes. The coconut milk solids build a thick, stable head with hot water and thirty seconds of stirring. One sachet makes one cup, and nothing else is needed — no dairy, no frother, no machine.

Is this just instant coffee?

No. Most instant is bulked out with chicory, milk powder and sugar. Romoire is single origin Arabica with coconut milk and monk fruit, and nothing else. It dissolves as fast as instant. That is where the resemblance ends.

What does monk fruit taste like?

Clean and rounded, without the metallic finish some sweeteners leave. A monk fruit coffee takes its sweetness from a plant extract rather than sugar, so it sits behind the coffee instead of in front of it. It isn't sugar and doesn't pretend to be.

Does it contain any dairy?

None. No milk powder, no milk solids, no lactose. The body and the froth come from coconut milk, which is why this is a dairy free coffee premix rather than an ordinary one with the milk taken out. [Add the dairy-free facility line only if the line is dedicated.]

Where does the coffee come from?

One place: Chikmagalur, in the hills of Karnataka. Single origin Arabica, sourced and made in India — the beans, the formulation and the production.
